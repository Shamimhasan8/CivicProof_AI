# Responsible AI

The product name describes the intended platform; current AI inference is local Tesseract OCR. Explanations, retrieval and letters are deterministic. No provider LLM is connected.

Rules are candidate mappings of published clauses, not legal eligibility decisions. All await specialist review. Unknown requests, negations, sensitive demands and unresolved correction cases abstain. Absence of a requirement in this collection does not establish illegality. Similar names are never treated as the same identity by fuzzy matching. Consistency does not authenticate documents. A discrepancy does not establish fraud.

Bilingual keyword retrieval has limited semantic understanding. Complex or compound requests can exceed its capability; official clarification is needed. OCR errors and transliteration differences require manual review. The benchmark is synthetic and developer-labeled; no generalization to real legal cases is claimed.

The citizen controls event recording, letter editing, exports and all external actions. No email, complaint or government application is automatically submitted. There are no legal accusations, promised outcomes, invented partnerships, fake users or measured token-saving claims.
