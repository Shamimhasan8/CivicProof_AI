# International design research

Retrieved 15 November 2026. These sources inform interface patterns only; CivicProof has no integration or affiliation with them.

| Reference | Observed design principle | CivicProof adaptation |
|---|---|---|
| [UK Passport Check and Send](https://www.gov.uk/how-the-post-office-check-and-send-service-works) | Application preparation and supporting-document checking do not guarantee approval. | Document preflight with explicit non-eligibility language. |
| [USCIS service tools](https://egov.uscis.gov/office-locator/serv) | Online account supports case status and history. | Personal evidence timeline, clearly separated from official status. |
| [Estonia data tracker](https://e-estonia.com/digitalisation-myths/) | Citizens can inspect data-access logs. | Source lineage and citizen-visible record labels; no equivalent government audit access claimed. |
| [European Commission SOLVIT](https://ec.europa.eu/solvit/how-solvit-works/index_en.htm) | Administrative problem-solving through designated channels. | Respectful clarification pathway and external GRS guidance; no SOLVIT service offered. |
| [Australian GovCMS Rules as Code](https://www.govcms.gov.au/dxp/rules-as-code) | Explicit computational conditions and inspectable rule application. | Small versioned conditional mappings, held as drafts pending specialist review. |
| [DigiLocker FAQ](https://wb.digilocker.gov.in/web/about/faq) | User-uploaded copies and documents from original issuers have distinct provenance. | Citizen-reported / uploaded records never presented as authenticated institutional records. |

Bangladesh requirements are exclusively based on the Bangladesh registry in DATA_PROVENANCE.md. International patterns do not create or override Bangladesh administrative requirements.
